# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project participants must communicate professionally, focus criticism on the
work, respect differing experience and viewpoints, and avoid harassment,
threats, discrimination, or disclosure of another person's private
information.

Security-sensitive discussions require additional care. Never pressure a
participant to disclose recovery material, private credentials, personal
information, or unpublished vulnerability details.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private communications or personal information without
  permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, unsafe instructions, or secret
  material.
- Publicly disclosing an unpatched vulnerability before maintainers have had
  a reasonable opportunity to respond.
- Disrupting issues, reviews, or support channels in bad faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to
`security@protocolwealthllc.com`. Do not include production recovery material
or unrelated personal information. Ordinary disagreements about code should
remain in the relevant issue or pull request.

This policy applies in project repositories, review channels, and other
project-managed spaces.
